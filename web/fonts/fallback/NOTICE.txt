Fallback fonts for the Flutter web engine, self-hosted so the app makes no
requests to Google at runtime. Same paths as https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/
(see web/flutter_bootstrap.js fontFallbackBaseUrl and
tool/fetch_fallback_fonts.py).

- Noto fonts (Noto Sans *, Noto Color Emoji, Noto Music, ...):
  SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://openfontlicense.org
  Source: https://github.com/notofonts
- Roboto: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (earlier releases Apache License 2.0),
  https://github.com/googlefonts/roboto-3-classic
